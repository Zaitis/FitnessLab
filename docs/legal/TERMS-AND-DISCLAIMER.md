## 1. What FitnessLab is

It is **not** a commercial product, not a medical device, and not a health
service. It is not operated by a company, and there is no support desk
behind it.

## 2. No professional qualifications, no medical advice

The person who built FitnessLab is a software developer. They are **not a
doctor, dietitian, nutritionist, physiotherapist, or certified personal
trainer**, and hold no qualification in medicine, nutrition, or fitness.

Everything this application produces — BMI values, weight categories,
training plans, meal plans, and calorie figures — is generated by simple,
hard-coded rules written to demonstrate programming techniques. These outputs:

- are **not medical, nutritional, dietary, or fitness advice**;
- are **not tailored** to your health status, medical history, medication,
  injuries, allergies, or individual circumstances;
- have **not been reviewed or approved** by any qualified professional;
- must **not** be used to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition.

**Consult a qualified healthcare professional before changing your diet,
starting an exercise programme, or acting on anything you see here.**

## 3. Limitations of BMI

BMI is a crude population-level statistic, not an individual health
assessment. It is calculated only from height and weight, and therefore
cannot distinguish muscle from fat, or account for body composition, frame
size, fat distribution, age, or ethnicity.

BMI is particularly unreliable, and its categories should not be applied, for:

- children and adolescents under 18, for whom age- and sex-specific growth
  charts are required instead;
- people who are pregnant or breastfeeding;
- athletes and people with high muscle mass, who are routinely misclassified
  as overweight;
- older adults, and people with conditions affecting body composition or
  fluid balance.

A BMI figure alone says nothing conclusive about any individual's health.

## 4. Please take care of yourself

If you have, or think you may have, an eating disorder or a difficult
relationship with food, weight, or exercise, tools that display weight
categories and calorie targets can be harmful. This application is a
programming demonstration and is not designed with any clinical safeguards.
Please consider not using it, and speak to a healthcare professional or a
support organisation in your country.

Do not use this application to restrict calories below levels a qualified
professional has advised, and stop exercising and seek medical attention if
you experience pain, dizziness, chest discomfort, or breathing difficulty.

## 5. Intended users

FitnessLab is intended for adults aged 18 or over. Accounts should not be
created by anyone under 18. As noted above, BMI categories are not valid for
under-18s in any case.

## 7. Your account and your data

Creating an account is optional — the BMI calculator on the landing page
works without one, and calculations made there are not stored on the server.

If an account is created, the data stored is: name, email address, password
(stored only as a secure hash), preferred language, and the measurements,
plans, and check-offs created within the application.

**What this means under the GDPR.** Body weight and BMI, linked to an
identifiable person, may be treated as data concerning health — a special
category under Article 9. This application handles them on the basis of your
explicit consent, given when you choose to create an account and enter them.
Consent can be withdrawn at any time by deleting your account, which removes
the account and all data associated with it. Data is stored on a server in
the European Union and is not sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.

You may request access to your data, its correction, or its deletion by
contacting the address in section 12. The simplest route is to delete your
account from the application, which does the same thing immediately.

Separately from your account, the application keeps short-lived technical
error logs so that faults can be diagnosed. These may incidentally include an
email address or an identifier if an error occurred while you were using the
site. Passwords and access tokens are stripped before anything is written,
the logs are readable only by the site administrator, and entries are deleted
automatically after a short retention period.

**The minimum-data principle applies to you too:** please do not enter more
than you need to try the application out. Nothing here requires real data.

Because this is a demonstration project, **no guarantee is made about data
retention, backups, or availability**. Data may be lost, and the demo may be
reset or taken offline without notice. Do not store anything here that
matters to you.
